About Naruto

Naruto Uzumaki is a shinobi of the Hidden Leaf Village and the son of Minato Namikaze and Kushina Uzumaki. As a child, Naruto struggled with loneliness and wanted to earn the respect and acknowledgment of his village.

Naruto possesses enormous chakra reserves and becomes the jinchuriki of the Nine-Tailed Fox. He develops many powerful techniques, including the Shadow Clone Jutsu and Rasengan, while training under several experienced shinobi.

Throughout his journey, Naruto learns the importance of friendship, perseverance and protecting others. His dream of becoming Hokage ultimately reflects his desire to be recognized and to protect the people he cares about.

Skills & Abilities

Naruto's most notable abilities.

Shadow Clone Jutsu

Naruto can create multiple physical clones using chakra, allowing him to overwhelm opponents and train efficiently.

Rasengan

Naruto masters the Rasengan, a powerful spinning chakra technique that becomes one of his signature attacks.

Nine-Tails Chakra

Naruto can access the immense chakra of the Nine-Tailed Fox, greatly increasing his combat power and endurance.
