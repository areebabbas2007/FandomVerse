About Kirito

Kirito is the main protagonist of Sword Art Online. He becomes trapped inside the virtual MMORPG Sword Art Online and must survive its dangerous challenges while searching for a way to return to the real world.

As a skilled player, Kirito develops a reputation for his remarkable swordsmanship and fast reactions. His experience as a solo player allows him to adapt quickly to difficult battles and unfamiliar situations.

Throughout his adventures, Kirito forms strong bonds with other players and repeatedly places himself in danger to protect them. His determination and loyalty remain central parts of his character.
