About Killua

Killua Zoldyck

Killua Zoldyck is one of the central characters of Hunter × Hunter. He comes from the famous Zoldyck family and was trained from a young age as an assassin.

Despite his background, Killua develops a strong friendship with Gon Freecss. Their journey together allows Killua to discover a life beyond the expectations placed on him by his family.

Killua is known for his intelligence, incredible speed, combat skills and distinctive lightning-based abilities.

What Makes Killua Special?

Lightning

Killua can transform his aura into electricity and use it during combat.

Speed

His assassin training gives him remarkable speed and reflexes.

Intelligence

Killua is highly observant and can quickly analyze dangerous situations.

Assassin Training

Years of training have made Killua highly capable in hand-to-hand combat.

Killua's journey is about discovering friendship, freedom and his own identity.
