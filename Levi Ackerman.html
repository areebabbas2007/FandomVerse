About Levi

Levi Ackerman is one of the central soldiers of Attack on Titan. He serves as a captain in the Survey Corps and is widely recognized for his extraordinary skill with the Vertical Maneuvering Equipment.

Levi is known for his serious, disciplined and composed personality. Despite his strict nature, he cares deeply about his comrades and takes responsibility for the people under his command.

His fighting ability, speed and precise movements make him one of the most formidable soldiers in the Survey Corps.
